Our Studio Philosophy

Tools built for a lifetime of hearth, table, and morning rituals.

Kettle & Crate was founded in Portland, Oregon out of a quiet frustration with disposable cookware, synthetic coatings, and fragile kitchenware designed for landfills rather than generations.

The Value of Permanent Goods

We believe the objects you touch every morning ought to possess honest weight, natural materials, and quiet endurance. A cast iron dutch oven should outlast its original owner, passing down a seasoned non-stick patina from parent to child. A morning coffee mug should feel warm, grounded, and human in the palm, thrown on an artisan potter's wheel rather than extruded from industrial plastic molds.

In our early years exploring workshops across the Pacific Northwest, Alsace, and central Japan, we met family-owned foundries and fourth-generation ceramicists still practicing time-honored methods. They were not chasing quarterly trends or synthetic shortcuts. Instead, they focused on thermal density, natural mineral glazes, and metallurgical purity. That unhurried dedication became the foundation of Kettle & Crate.

Direct Maker Partnerships

Rather than relying on sprawling distributors and anonymous supply chains, we work directly with independent makers. Our enameled cookware is sand-cast in small batches in France, finished with double-fired porcelain glazes that resist chipping and thermal stress. Our tableware is shaped from iron-rich stoneware clays and wood-fired to 2,200°F in Coimbra and Hood River.

By maintaining close relationships with our workshop partners, we guarantee honest pricing, dignified craft wages, and uncompromising quality standards. We visit our partner foundries annually, refine handle ergonomics together, and evaluate prototypes in real working kitchens for months before offering them to you.

Responsible Stewardship

A permanent kitchen tool is inherently sustainable because it eliminates the cycle of constant replacement. But we hold ourselves to the same standard in our packaging and fulfillment.

Every single parcel dispatched from our Portland fulfillment studio is completely free of expanded polystyrene foam, plastic air pillows, and vinyl packing tape. We package your provisions in unbleached, FSC-certified kraft corrugate boxes, cushioned with recycled molded paper pulp and sealed with water-activated paper tape. Everything you receive can be placed directly into curbside paper recycling or home compost.
