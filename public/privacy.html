Privacy Policy

Customer Privacy Policy

Last revised: September 1, 2026 · Studio standards

At Kettle & Crate, we respect the quiet sanctuary of your home. We treat your personal information with the same care and honest standards that we bring to our kitchen tools. We do not sell, rent, or monetize customer data to third-party data brokers or advertising networks.

1. Information We Collect

When you create an account, purchase studio provisions, or write to our workshop, we collect necessary transactional information: your name, email address, physical delivery address, and order transaction history. Payment card numbers are tokenized by our payment processor and are never stored on our application servers.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your personal data strictly to fulfill orders, calculate regional shipping rates, generate delivery tracking labels, process refunds, and communicate regarding customer care requests. If you explicitly subscribe to our Morning Dispatch newsletter, we send occasional studio essays and kiln release updates. You can unsubscribe with one click at any time.

3. Information Sharing with Service Partners

We share your information solely with essential service partners required to complete fulfillment: postal couriers (USPS, UPS) to deliver your parcels, and payment gateways to authorize transactions. These partners are legally bound to handle your data securely and exclusively for providing fulfillment services.

4. Data Retention and Your Rights

We retain order history records as required for legitimate accounting, tax compliance, and warranty verification. You may request access to, correction of, or permanent deletion of your customer account profile at any time by contacting privacy@kettleandcrate.com.

5. Security Practices

Account passwords are encrypted using salted scrypt hashing algorithms. All sessions transmit over secure HTTPS transport protocols. We maintain strict internal access controls to protect your information against unauthorized disclosure.
