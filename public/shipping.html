Our 30-Day Home Trial Policy

Cooking is an intimate daily ritual. You cannot truly evaluate a skillet's balance or a mug's lip until you hold it over morning coffee or sear a meal at your own stove.

If for any reason a tool does not meet your expectations, you may return it within 30 days of receipt for a complete refund. We ask only that returned items be in clean, resalable condition with their original product boxes where applicable.

How to Initiate a Return

  1. Email our customer studio at returns@kettleandcrate.com with your order number.
  2. We will provide a prepaid domestic return shipping label via email within one business day.
  3. Drop the sealed parcel off at any USPS post office or blue collection box.
  4. Once received at our studio, your refund will be processed to your original payment method within 48 hours.

Damaged or Transit-Impacted Deliveries

Despite our molded paper packaging, rough courier transit occasionally impacts ceramics or glassware. If any item arrives chipped, fractured, or defective, send a photo to studio@kettleandcrate.com. We will immediately dispatch an expedited replacement at zero cost to you, with no requirement to package or return the broken piece.
